How units work
  • Units are your slices of the fund.
  • Value per unit (shown as NAV) = fund value ÷ total units.
  • Your value = your units × value per unit.
  • Adding money buys units at that day's value, so it doesn't change the value of anyone else's units.
  • A transfer moves units between members. The fund's total stays the same.
  • After a transfer, your gain compares your value with what your current units cost you. Gains from units already sold show as "realized".
